Why masonry

Built once. Built to stay.

Lasts generations

Brick, block and stone stand up to weather, pests and time. Masonry buildings routinely outlive the ones around them.

Saves energy

Masonry's mass holds heat in winter and keeps it out in summer, which trims heating and cooling bills year after year.

Won't burn

Brick and block do not burn. That is real protection for the people and property inside.

Looks right

Any color, texture or bond pattern, and it only looks better with age.

Low upkeep

No painting, no sealing, no rot. A masonry wall asks for very little.

Easy on the earth

Made from clay, sand and stone, and built to be built once.
