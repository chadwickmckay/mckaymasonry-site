How a bid works
  1. Send the drawings.
    Plans, specs, addenda and the bid date, to the email on this page. A link to your plan room works too.
  2. We do a detailed takeoff.
    Every wall measured, every unit counted.
  3. You get a clear proposal.
    What is in, what is out and one number you can carry.
  4. Homeowners:
    a few photos, rough measurements and your town are enough to start.
